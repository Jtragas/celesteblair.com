Celeste Blair

Artist · Advocate · Author · Mentor

Creator of the Peer Success Reentry Collective. Presidential clemency recipient. Prison Professors Ambassador. Dedicated to empowering justice-impacted individuals through art, mentorship, and the belief that every person deserves a second chance.

July 1, 2026

Return to Aliceville

The Peer Success Team welcomes Celeste Blair back to FCI Aliceville

On July 1, 2026, Celeste returned to FCI Aliceville as a free woman, invited by facility leadership to speak at a celebration honoring the Peer Success Team she created while incarcerated. It is believed to be the first time a clemency recipient has been invited back to speak at the facility where they served, while still on home confinement.
